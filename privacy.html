Ates Fighter

Privacy Policy · Last updated: November 23, 2026

In short: Ates Fighter does not collect, store or share any personal data.

Data we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not connect to the internet and does not send any information to us or to third parties.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your settings (language, difficulty, sound and vibration), your high score and your saved game on your device only. This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app removes it.

Children

Because the game collects no personal data, it does not collect data from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
