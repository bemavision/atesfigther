Contact us
Questions, problems or feedback? Email bemavision.support@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days. Please mention your device model and the game version (shown at the bottom of the main menu).

How do I play?

Move your ship with the joystick on the left. Hold FIRE to shoot and tap ROCKET to launch a homing rocket. Reach the end of each mission and defeat the boss. Spend the credits you earn in the Hangar on weapons, rockets, lives, shields and upgrades.

How do I save my progress?

The game saves automatically at the end of every mission. You can also save in the middle of a mission from the pause menu. Use Load Game on the main menu to continue.

Can I change the difficulty?

Yes, in Settings, before you start a new game. There are seven levels, from Newbie to Brutal. The campaign route shows the difficulty you cleared each mission on.

How do I beat the Red Shield?

Its shield is powered by two generators on its arms. Destroy both generators first; then the shield drops and the ship takes damage.

Does the game need an internet connection, or contain ads or purchases?

No. Ates Fighter plays fully offline. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.
